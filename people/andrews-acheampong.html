About

Andrews Acheampong is an MPhil researcher in Biodata Analytics and Computational Genomics at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. He has a background in Statistics and is interested in developing statistical and computational methods for complex biological and genomic data.

His research interests include Bayesian statistics, statistical genomics, high-dimensional biological data, computational statistics, bioinformatics, and statistical methods for molecular biology and genomics. He is particularly interested in developing statistical approaches for complex genomic data and improving the interpretation of biological information.

Current Research

Andrews' current research focuses on developing statistical methods for high-dimensional biological and genomic data. His work explores Bayesian modeling, statistical genomics, and the use of biological information to improve the analysis and interpretation of complex biological outcomes.
