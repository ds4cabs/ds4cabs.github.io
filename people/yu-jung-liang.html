About

Hello, I'm Ambery Liang, a Master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC San Diego, specializing in Machine Learning and Data Science. My core expertise lies in developing and deploying advanced computational architectures to transform complex healthcare data into actionable clinical intelligence.

My most significant experience comes from my role as an AI Engineer at Far Eastern Memorial Hospital.I architected a novel, end-to-end AI framework for chronic disease management. This system features two major components:

First, a predictive module utilizes a temporal GNN framework (integrating Time-Aware Transformers and GraphMamba) trained on a decade of longitudinal EHR data. This model captures irregular temporal dependencies to accurately forecast complex disease trajectories.

Second, the system incorporates a Multi-Agent Clinical Decision Support System. This component leverages LLMs (via LangGraph) to process unstructured physician inputs, querying a Neo4j knowledge graph to generate personalized and explainable treatment recommendations , thereby bridging the gap between historical data and actionable future insights.

Beyond this, I am skilled in diverse deep learning architectures, including U-Net and YOLOv8 for medical imaging analysis , and possess strong proficiency in PyTorch, Python, and graph learning techniques (GNNs).
