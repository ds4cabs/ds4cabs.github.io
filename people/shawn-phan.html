About

My name is Shawn Phan, and I am a Data Science student at the University of San Francisco. I am interested in programming, data analysis, and artificial intelligence, particularly how these technologies can be applied to real-world problems.

Internship Work

During my internship, I worked on OpenLedger, an AI-powered project designed to make pharmaceutical and healthcare data easier to access and analyze. My work involved developing and testing Python tools that retrieve and process data from sources such as SEC EDGAR, Drugs@FDA, the FDA Orange Book, CMS NADAC, DailyMed, and RxNorm.

I also worked with the Gemini API to extract information from pharmaceutical company financial filings and supported the development and testing of a Streamlit-based dashboard that brings together information about products, revenue, FDA data, pricing, exclusivity, and drug labeling.

Future Plans

Moving forward, I plan to continue studying Data Science at the University of San Francisco while further developing my skills in Python, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data analysis. I am especially interested in learning more about how AI systems can work with large amounts of real-world data and support research and decision-making.

In the future, I hope to pursue a career related to data science, artificial intelligence, or software development. My experience working on OpenLedger gave me a better understanding of how these technologies can be applied to real-world industries and provided valuable experience working on a larger technical project.
