Past intern cohorts

The CABS Data Science Intern Program offers focused 10-week summer and 2-week winter sessions aimed at cultivating data science skills among undergraduate students, with a particular emphasis on the biopharmaceutical industry. Interns work on real-world projects with professional mentorship from CABS members, building both technical data skills and hands-on knowledge of the biopharma industry.
