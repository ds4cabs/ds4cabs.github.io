About

Nicole Lee is a junior at Amador Valley High School in Pleasanton, California, with a strong interest in medicine, neuroscience, patient care, and healthcare communication. She has maintained a rigorous academic schedule, while pursuing advanced coursework in biology, chemistry, biomedical science, and human body systems.

Nicole's passion for healthcare was inspired by her family's experience with Parkinson's disease and has grown through her volunteer work with Parkinson’s Networking & Movement Disorders (PNMD), where she supports patients, caregivers, and community programs. In Summer 2026, she was selected to participate in the UCSF Movement Disorders & Neuromodulation Center Clinical Shadowing Program, gaining firsthand exposure to neurology, multidisciplinary patient care, and movement disorders medicine.

Beyond healthcare, Nicole is actively engaged in leadership and community service. She founded CNCN (Chronic NeuroCare Network), which supports education and awareness for neurological conditions. She is also a volunteer at the Oakland Zoo, a member of HOSA and Biology Club, and a swimming coach for younger students.

A lifelong artist, Nicole has studied visual arts since childhood under French artist Cécile Girard and is currently an AP Art student exploring the intersection of art, neuroscience, and patient-centered communication. She is also a varsity tennis player and holds an American Red Cross Lifeguarding Certification.

Nicole hopes to pursue a future career in medicine, combining scientific knowledge, compassionate patient care, leadership, and creative communication to improve the lives of others.
