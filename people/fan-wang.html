About

Fan Wang is a biostatistician, statistical geneticist, and data science educator with a PhD in Statistical Sciences from the University of Toronto. Fan develops statistical and computational methods for understanding complex biomedical data, with a focus on statistical genetics, multi-omics integration, and high-dimensional data analysis.

Fan's research experience includes postdoctoral research in the Department of Biostatistics at Columbia University and PhD research at The Hospital for Sick Children. Research projects span genome-wide association studies (GWAS), colocalization, genomic signal-region detection, and uncertainty quantification for polygenic risk prediction.

Mentoring interests

My mentoring interests include statistical genetics and multi-omics integration for disease-mechanism discovery and candidate target prioritization; predictive modeling and uncertainty quantification for precision health; and reproducible biomedical data science.

I enjoy helping students formulate research questions, develop and validate statistical methods, build reproducible analysis workflows, and communicate findings clearly. Potential projects include GWAS/eQTL integration, health risk prediction, and open-source analytical tools using R or Python.

Teaching & mentorship

At the University of Toronto's Data Sciences Institute, Fan teaches and develops applied data science courses, including Genetic Data Analysis Toolbox, and has taught Data Visualization. Teaching experience also includes graduate statistical and computational methods and quantitative genomics workshops at Columbia University.

Fan has mentored doctoral researchers on statistical modeling, high-dimensional signal detection, and genomic data integration. Instruction combines conceptual explanations, real-data examples, and hands-on coding to support learners from varied quantitative backgrounds.

Selected open-source work

  • Regenie.QRS ↗

    Computationally efficient quantile regression for genome-wide association studies of quantitative traits, accounting for genetic relatedness at biobank scale.

  • Simple Sum 2 Colocalization ↗

    A method that combines GWAS and eQTL summary statistics across gene–tissue pairs to assess colocalization, with support for overlapping study participants and meta-analysis data.

  • LocusFocus ↗

    A web-based platform for GWAS follow-up and colocalization with molecular association data, incorporating colocalization methodology developed during Fan's doctoral research.
