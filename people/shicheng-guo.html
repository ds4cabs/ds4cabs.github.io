About

Shicheng Guo is Senior Director of Translational Genetics and Data Science at Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, where he leads the development of AI-integrated decision-making frameworks across drug discovery and development — turning large-scale human genetic and clinical datasets into actionable insight for target identification, patient stratification, and clinical planning.

His work sits at the intersection of statistical genetics, multi-omics, and machine learning, with a current focus on operationalizing AI in biomedical R&D: moving from one-off AI tools toward scalable, trusted systems that drive real-world scientific and clinical decisions. Within CABS, he serves as a DS4CABS program leader and co-chairs the IT & Data Science Committee, mentoring the next generation of data scientists in biopharma.

Selected contributions

Dr. Guo's research spans the genetic, epigenetic, and transcriptional regulation of human disease — including cancer, autoimmune disorders, and hemochromatosis. Among his notable contributions is a gene-based recessive diplotype approach that identified FGF6 as a novel hemochromatosis gene (published in Blood, 2019), along with extensive work on DNA-methylation biomarkers for cancer diagnostics. His full publication record is available via his personal site and Google Scholar.
