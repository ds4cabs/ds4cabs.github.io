About

I am a graduate student in the Chemistry and Chemical Biology Department at the University of California, Davis, specializing in computational chemistry and molecular modeling. My research focuses on mechanistic studies of terpene biosynthesis using quantum mechanical calculations, with an emphasis on how molecular structure and energy landscapes influence reactivity and product outcomes.

I have experience applying DFT and high-level electronic structure methods to model reaction pathways and analyze energy barriers. In addition, I have studied protein–ligand interactions using molecular docking, integrating structure-based design with mechanistic insight to better understand reactivity and selectivity in biologically relevant systems.
